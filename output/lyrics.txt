## Song Title: **“Don’t Frown at My Gift”**

### Lyrics

**Verse 1**  
I rode the north wind through the dark and landed on your street,  
I carried boxes, ribbons, bows, and snow beneath my feet.  
I checked the list twice, wrote your name, I found the perfect thing,  
A red toy train that sang a tune and a starry winter swing.  

**Chorus**  
Oh, don’t frown at my gift, don’t sigh on Christmas morn,  
I crossed the frozen midnight just to make your holiday warm.  
The elves are hiding faces, the reindeer say, “Oh dear,”  
Santa’s mad because you didn’t like what was waiting here.  

**Verse 2**  
The tree was bright, the fire crackled, stockings lined the wall,  
I watched you open up the box and watched you shrug and stall.  
You said, “I wanted something else,” and set it on the floor,  
I turned around to hide my face and held my breath once more.  

**Chorus**  
Oh, don’t frown at my gift, don’t sigh on Christmas morn,  
I crossed the frozen midnight just to make your holiday warm.  
The elves are hiding faces, the reindeer say, “Oh dear,”  
Santa’s mad because you didn’t like what was waiting here.  

**Verse 3**  
I know you have a wish for more, for toys and dreams and plans,  
But wrapping takes a lot of time and love for little hands.  
So say a thank you softly, let the grumpiness release,  
Santa’s mad because a frown can spoil his Christmas peace.  

**Final Chorus**  
Oh, don’t frown at my gift, don’t sigh on Christmas morn,  
I crossed the frozen midnight just to make your holiday warm.  
The elves are hiding faces, the reindeer say, “Oh dear,”  
Santa’s mad because you didn’t like what was waiting here.  

**Tag**  
Ho, ho, hmm… be glad it’s Christmas, kid.  
Ho, ho, hmm… Santa’s still mad.

---

## Music Description

**Style:**  
A humorous, family-friendly vintage Christmas novelty pop song. It should feel like a playful mix of classic holiday jingles and a slightly grumpy rockabilly/swing tune.

**Tempo:**  
Around **108–112 BPM**, with a light swing or shuffle feel. Not too fast; it should have a sauntering, “Santa is walking around with his hands on his hips” groove.

**Time Signature:**  
**4/4** with a swing feel, or **12/8** for a more traditional Christmas shuffle.

**Key:**  
**G major** is a good key for a warm, singable holiday song. The chorus can lean toward **C major** and **D7** to feel bright but a little dramatic when Santa starts complaining.

**Overall Mood:**  
Festive, funny, mock-angry, and catchy. Santa is not frightening — he is more exasperated than scary, like a grumpy uncle at Christmas morning.

---

## Instrumentation

- **Sleigh bells** throughout the song, tight on the beat.
- **Glockenspiel or celesta** for a sparkling Christmas texture.
- **Upright bass or electric bass with a walking swing line** to give Santa a sneaky, playful rhythm.
- **Acoustic piano** with bright, staccato chords.
- **Brushed snare drums** in the verses, then fuller drums in the chorus.
- **Electric guitar** with light tremolo and twangy Christmas rockabilly lines.
- **Handclaps** on the chorus to make it feel like everyone is joining in.
- **Brass hits** for emphasis on “Santa’s mad” and “ho ho hmm.”
- Optional subtle sound effects:
  - A tiny **reindeer hoof-stomp** sound in the second chorus.
  - A comedic **“elf gasp”** or small choir “ooh” after “the reindeer say, oh dear.”
  - A final **bell ring** before the tag.

---

## Vocal Style

The lead vocal should sound warm, deep, and slightly grumbly — a friendly baritone or alto voice. It should not be angry in a scary way; more like Santa trying very hard to be polite but clearly irritated.

- Verses: almost conversational, sneaky, and restrained.
- Chorus: more melodic, full, and singable.
- Backup vocals: children’s choir, family voices, or a small group saying “ho ho hmm” in the tag.
- Santa’s “ho ho hmm” should be delivered with a comedic pause, almost like he cannot decide whether to laugh or be annoyed.

---

## Suggested Chord Progression

**Verse:**  
G – Em – C – D

**Chorus:**  
C – G/B – Am – D  
Then end on:  
G – D – C – G

This keeps the song cheerful but gives Santa’s lines a little dramatic lift.

---

## Arrangement Plan

**Intro**  
4 bars of sleigh bells, glockenspiel, and bass. A soft piano riff introduces the swing feel.

**Verse 1**  
Light and sneaky: just sleigh bells, bass, piano, and voice. Maybe one guitar pluck in the background.

**Chorus 1**  
Fuller: drums, handclaps, brass hits, and group “oh, don’t frown at my gift” backing vocal.

**Verse 2**  
A little more playful: add tambourine and guitar stabs. Keep the “I watched you open up the box” line dramatic, like Santa is remembering something embarrassing.

**Chorus 2**  
Same as first chorus, maybe with a higher harmony line and stronger brass.

**Verse 3**  
The mood softens slightly because the song starts giving advice. The music becomes warmer: piano, strings, glockenspiel, and a gentle vocal.

**Final Chorus**  
Bigger and more celebratory. Add group vocals and a final brass flourish.

**Outro**  
The music slows down just a little. Santa delivers the tag with comedic timing:

“Ho, ho, hmm… be glad it’s Christmas, kid.”  
One bell rings.  
“Ho, ho, hmm… Santa’s still mad.”  
Final sleigh-bell shake ends the song.
